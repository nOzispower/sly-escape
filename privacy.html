Privacy Policy

Sly Escape · effective October 18, 2026

For kids, the short version: Sly Escape does not ask who you are, and it does not send anything about you anywhere. Your game progress stays on your device. There are no ads.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal information. No names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, or advertising IDs.
  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Sly Escape has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No chat or social features. Players cannot talk to each other or share anything.
  • Saves stay on your device. Progress, coins, unlocked items and sound settings are stored only on the device you play on.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Sly Escape keeps a small save file in your device's local app storage: which levels you finished, your stars and coins, the items you unlocked, the head and chaser you picked, and whether sound and music are on. A backup copy is kept on the device in case a save gets damaged. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data for the web version) deletes it.

The web version may also keep an offline copy of the game files on your device so it can be played without an internet connection. These are only game files, not information about you.

In-app purchases

Sly Escape can be played for free. It offers optional in-app purchases of game coins. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or receive your name, payment card or billing details; the store only tells the game that a purchase was completed so the coins can be added.

A grown-up check (a parental gate) appears before any purchase and before any link that leaves the game. Parents can also turn off or limit purchases with Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link.

Children's privacy

Sly Escape is made for children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not collect it from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). This app is designed to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR (including its rules for children), Apple's Kids Category guidelines and Google Play's Families policies.

Web version hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the website, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself does not collect, store or send this information.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You control all game data on your device and can remove it at any time by deleting the app or clearing site data. If you have any questions, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first, and for children, without any consent the law requires.
