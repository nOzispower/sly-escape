Need help?

Email slyescape@mail.grokbot.com. Please tell us your device (for example "iPhone 13" or "Pixel 7"), the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings), and what happened. Grown-ups, please send the email for your child. We never need your child's name or any personal details.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag anywhere to move your hungry head. Gobble things smaller than you to grow, reach the goal before time runs out, and don't let the chaser catch you! Open How to Play from the main menu for the full guide.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. It is not linked to an account, so deleting the app or clearing the browser's data removes it, and progress does not move between devices.

How do I get coins? Win levels, with bonus coins for stars and combos. Coins unlock farm worlds, theme maps, heads and chasers. Grown-ups can also buy optional coin packs.

I bought something. Where is it? Coins are added right after the store confirms the purchase. If the one-time Starter Pack is missing, open Settings → Restore Purchases. Coin packs are used up when you spend them, so they cannot be restored.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (Play refund help).

How can I stop purchases? Every purchase is behind a grown-up check. You can also turn off in-app purchases with Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link.

Sound or music? Use the 🔊 and 🎵 buttons in a level, or Settings.
