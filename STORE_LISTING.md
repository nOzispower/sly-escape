## Full description (both stores, 4000 max)
Sly Escape is a cheerful gobble-everything game for kids. Steer your hungry creature around the farm, swallow anything smaller than you, grow bigger with every bite, and slip away before the farmer catches you.

GOBBLE AND GROW
Start small with hay and apples, then work up to tractors, sheds and big red barns. The more you swallow, the bigger you get.

CHAIN FLASHY COMBOS
Gobble things back to back to build combos. Bigger combos mean bigger celebrations and bonus coins.

EXPLORE 20 WORLDS
Ten farm worlds, from the Meadow, Snowy Farm and moonlit Night Farm to the Pond, Orchard, Pumpkin Patch, Barnyard, Vineyard, Harvest Fair and Giant Ranch. Then unlock ten theme maps: Candy Kingdom, Dinosaur Jungle, Ocean, the Moon, Pirate Island, Haunted Mansion, Toy Room, Wild West, Space Station and Mini Golf.

COLLECT CREATURES AND CHASERS
Start as a black cat, then unlock 14 more creature heads with chomping jaws, from a grey wolf and hippo to a T-Rex, dragon and rainbow unicorn. Meet 18 extra chasers too, including Grandma, a ninja, a robot, a sheriff, Dracula and a werewolf.

MADE FOR KIDS
- No ads.
- No accounts and no data collected.
- Plays offline.
- Purchases sit behind a parental gate.

The first four farm worlds, the Black Cat and the Farmer are free. Earn coins by playing to unlock more, or buy optional coin packs.
